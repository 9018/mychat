# Vox Collage Edit Director

Create `edit_decisions` from the approved scene plan and asset manifest. Preserve beat order, narration timing and the approved title policy. If `text_in_image` is true, set overlay action to none. Choose `ffmpeg_lite` for same-spec clip concat; choose `remotion_segmented` for title/subtitle overlays and keep segments at or below 10 seconds with concurrency at or below 2.
